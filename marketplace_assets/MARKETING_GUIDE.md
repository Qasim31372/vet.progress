## 🎯 1. Gumroad Setup (Fastest - 10 Minutes to First Listing)

1. **Sign Up:** Go to [Gumroad.com](https://gumroad.com) and create a seller account.
2. **Payout Setup:** Under **Settings > Payouts**, connect your **Payoneer** or direct bank account (available for Pakistan/international).
3. **Add Product:** Click **New Product**:
   * **Name:** `Vet Learn Hub - Full Veterinary Learning & Clinical Case Portal (Source Code)`
   * **Type:** Digital Product
   * **Price:** Start at **$29** (or $39 with a $10 launch discount).
4. **Product Description:** Open `marketplace_assets/PRODUCT_DESCRIPTION.md` and copy-paste the entire text into Gumroad's description field.
5. **Cover Graphic:** Create a 1280x720 cover in Canva using a laptop mockup showing the Student Dashboard.
6. **Upload Product File:** Create a `.zip` file of the project (excluding `node_modules` and `.git`) and upload it under the **Content** tab.
7. **Publish:** Hit **Publish**! Your sales link is now live.

## 🏆 2. CodeCanyon (Envato) Submission Checklist

CodeCanyon requires a specific zip bundle layout:

### Zip File Structure for Envato:
Prepare a zip named `main-file.zip` containing:
```text
main-file/
├── source-code/          # Clean zip of the codebase (without node_modules)
├── documentation/        # The documentation/ folder with index.html
└── licensing/            # Envato license text file
```

### Envato Listing Details:
* **Category:** `PHP Scripts` or `JavaScript / Full Applications / Node.js`
* **Title:** `Vet Learn Hub - Veterinary Clinical Management & Student Learning SaaS`
* **Demo URL:** Enter your deployed Render URL (e.g. `https://vet-learn-hub.onrender.com`)
* **Price:** **$35 – $49** (Regular License) | **$199** (Extended License)
